Local Folder Access Required

Cedar is built on 100% local data ownership. Your entire blog is saved directly to a real folder on your computer—never in temporary browser cache.

To allow Cedar to create and manage blog folders directly on your disk, please open this page in Chrome, Edge or most Chromium-based browsers like Vivaldi.
